Clean beats perfect. Wash it and wipe the wheels the morning of, and skip the tire shine — it flares under direct light. Chips, patina, and a work-in-progress build all photograph fine; that's often the interesting part.

We reschedule at no cost. Overcast is actually ideal for paint, so I only call it off for rain or unsafe roads — and your deposit carries over to the new date.
